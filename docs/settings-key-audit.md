### What cannot move, and why

- Secondary and background colours: the Material shell derives every role from one seed so that every text and icon pair keeps its contrast (checked for all seeds); a free second and third colour would break that.
- Card radius and font size: the shape scale and the type scale are fixed by the specification the shell follows; a slider would give shapes and text the spec does not have.
- Background grid and scanlines are effects of the old window's own cyberpunk skin; the shell has no such layer.
